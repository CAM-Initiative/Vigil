## Singleton family and non-duplication

The governed property is **actual usable AI-service availability and functional quality under independently established, applicable continuity obligations**, including proportionate degraded operation, recovery, alternative access and termination transition. An outage, ordinary degradation, harm, popularity or reliance alone is not an admitted failure: there must be an applicable obligation, a material shortfall and an insufficient required response. This is an independently recognisable bounded family even without an invented second class.

Existing families: FF-0005 governs access and session states (explicitly excluding generic service unavailability); FF-0006 governs validity and persistence of continuation state; FF-0008 governs activation/effectiveness of protective controls; FF-0009 governs agency-preserving influence and heightened interaction-level duties; FF-0010 governs authority exercised through infrastructure. No parent accommodates general obligated service functionality without changing its invariant. FC-032, FC-048, FC-035/036, FC-083, FC-049, FC-087 and FC-059 are distinct; independent co-occurrence requires separate evidence.
