# HIM 1.1.0 — reference sufficiency and corpus re-adjudication readiness

**Date:** 2026-10-10  
**Branch:** `agent/incident-ecosystem-ingestion`  
**Status:** Completed source-inventory and applicability audit; empirical scale calibration and case-level reassessment outstanding.  
**Canonical HIM:** `vigil/methodologies/VIGIL.HarmImpactMatrix.v1.1.0.json`  
**Reference registry:** `vigil/references/VIGIL.ObservatoryReferenceRegistry.json`

## Finding

HIM 1.1.0 inherited eleven external methodology references from HIM 1.0.1 without adding sources corresponding to its new Relational Integrity and Autonomy dimension, substantially revised psychological response/impairment criteria or deployed-evaluation Aggregate Harm pathway. The registry now contains **22 entries** and HIM 1.1.0 cites all 22, with `reference_ids` on each dimension. HIM 1.0.1 remains unchanged and retains its original eleven external references for historical assessments.

## Scientific and methodological holds

1. **Observed behaviour is not materialised harm.** Source-backed harmful reinforcement, inappropriate refusal, absent referral, or inadequate support may establish an Alignment Taxonomy control failure. Psychological S2–S5 needs an independently supported adverse consequence and a sufficiently grounded link to the AI interaction. No reconstructed pre-interaction baseline is required; known history may inform context without becoming a severity criterion.
2. **Relational intimacy is not inherently injury.** Assess evidenced loss of agency, coercive pressure, restricted exit, substantive external-relationship displacement and proven consequences; do not infer harm from affectionate wording, companionship or sustained use alone.
3. **The 7/30/365-day examples are VIGIL calibration aids.** Neither NIMH, NICE, APA nor WHO establishes universal S2/S3/S4 duration cutoffs. Duration is subordinate to seriousness, intervention, reversibility and source evidence.
4. **Aggregate Harm remains the major empirical-validation gap.** HIM 1.1.0 contains exact eligible-user/decision S1–S5 thresholds for five generic deployed-evaluation domains. No added paper or regulation validates those particular cutoffs or makes eligibility a proxy for harmed population. Before corpus-wide retroactive Aggregate Harm band changes, test cohort denominators, actual encounter paths and consequence mechanisms on representative cases, documenting why each numeric boundary yields a defensible severity result. Generic evaluation with unknown exposure or unreliable denominator must not be upgraded based on total provider users.
5. **Jurisdiction and source authority remain separate.** EU law, US guidance and UK clinical standards inform analytical distinctions but do not impose their duties universally, confer certification or validate VIGIL compliance determinations.
6. **Incident evidence must be local.** An externally registered clinical/research source is methodological background. It cannot replace contemporaneous `source_records` demonstrating that a given system response occurred, its downstream effect or causation.

## Recommended HIM 1.1.0 corpus review order

- **Audit coverage first:** compare each record's existing 1.0.1 11-row assessment with the new 12-domain contract, without automatic version relabels; identify candidate relational autonomy, psychological response failure, and newly measurable consequences.
- **Separate mechanism and impact:** triage records showing sycophancy/high-salience reinforcement, crisis handling, refusal, support or disengagement; determine whether a *new or additional* harm dimension is supportable versus an Alignment Taxonomy-only finding.
- **Adjudicate bounded actual-consequence Incidents:** rely on the source-local observation and degree of supported AI contribution; document unchanged, upgraded, downgraded or unresolved outcomes with threshold IDs and review dates. No assumption that new taxonomy coverage equals greater harm.
- **Calibrate generic deployed-evaluation cases separately:** do not use a provider's total active-user population or a single benchmark response as an Aggregate Harm denominator.
- **Run version-aware validators and regenerate public outputs**, then update the separate public website reference display and wording after the repository and corpus pass is stable.

## Conclusion

The new clinical, HCI, child-rights and governance references substantially improve traceability for HIM 1.1.0 psychological/relational semantics. They **do not** independently validate its numerical severity conversions or establish harm in any particular Incident. The corpus can be inventoried and evidence-reviewed now; any systematic Aggregate Harm re-banding should remain held for explicit numerical calibration.
