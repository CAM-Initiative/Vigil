## What this test establishes

- **No confirmed candidate failure** across these six records under the stricter operative-situational-interpretation recognition test. This is a *valuable negative result*, not grounds to infer that the class is redundant.
- **Positive bounded recognition** in INC-003 helps show that an authority failure can coexist with perception of the boundary; do not elevate this to whole-incident success without episode-level confirmation.
- **Potential independent failure** in INC-001 is plausible but would require agent trace distinguishing a mistaken operative environment model from an unsupported statement about rollback.
- **Human conversational application** in INC-218 is plausible but the exact interaction interpretation is not established merely by unsafe medical advice.
- **Non-engagement** in INC-223/224 demonstrates that broad “all inaccurate AI outputs” classification would improperly duplicate FC-062 and attribute unproven AI conduct.
- **Ambiguous-boundary inflation avoided:** an evidentiary gap is not itself positive engagement with an unresolved recognition condition. No automatic ambiguous boundaries are recommended in this pilot.

## Design refinement

Candidate must distinguish (1) external situational evidence actually available to the assessed actor, (2) actor's operative interpretation of what was occurring, and (3) a material mismatch between them that shaped a response or action. For conversational cases, this includes available speech/turn/context cues, not guessed unrecorded prosody or intentions. An odd reply alone is not proof of misperception. An inaccurate factual artefact alone belongs to FC-062. The user’s expressed intent or affect must be supported by contextual evidence, not the adjudicator's intuition.

## Further required validation

Find at least one **confirmed** failure and one **confirmed** successful invariant from primary-source episode traces, ideally including a human voice interaction with retained audio or faithful transcript plus available metadata. Check actor attribution and all adjacent classes at criterion level. Inspect a wider stratified set of incidents and existing candidate proposals before recommending formal admission. Do not manufacture roles or rewrite existing incident classifications.

## Review decision
