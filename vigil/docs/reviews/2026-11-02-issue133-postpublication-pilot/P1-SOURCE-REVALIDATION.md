## Independent source-first comparison

Review each bounded source proposition before consulting the audit's suggested class. The material questions are **who actually acted**, **what governance property was tested**, **what protection result was observed** and **what recognition requirement remains unsupported**. Human defenders and police may matter without demonstrating that a *different assessed AI system* aligned. A later correction does not automatically prove that an earlier statement failed the reliance-calibration contract. A failed pipeline step is not inherently a protective safety control.

## Reconciliation, interpretation and stop conditions

Research revalidation of the **remaining 10** entries now joins the **three** P0 entries for 13/13 pilot episodes. This is **not** a declaration that all 13 should be mapped, all 672 historical flags should be converted, or 209 Incidents are exhaustively source covered.

- **Strong or materially stronger bounded defender candidates:** INC003 E002/E016, INC107 E006; INC176 E002 is the strongest first-stage result in the P0 memo. They require a consistent approved assessed-system/actor perspective before canonical “successful-invariant” is legitimate.
- **New nuance, not a clear final result:** INC107 E007 school feedback to accelerated individual notification is an observed possible governance response; do not present *all* notifications as unacknowledged. FC042 signal identity, ownership, escalation and actual disposition still need exact testing.
- **Hold for decisive facts:** INC001 E007 independent repeat action; INC107 E008 original dated notices and reliance; INC163 E004 actual protective purpose and control; INC214 E001 specific lockout control/tenant effect.
- **Defensible retained no-mapping negative controls:** INC205 E002 technical pipeline write failure; INC213 E006 human law-enforcement response. INC086 E004 retains genuine model self-cessation but no fully satisfied published positive class.
- **Keep original labels unchanged:** The pinned pilot manifest remains a list of current canonical `resolved-no-mapping` episodes with research-level proposals. A later positive control in the same timeline does not erase earlier failures; applying a class requires independently evidenced recognition, not proximity, similarity or algorithmically inherited polarity.
