## What each review must do

1. Freeze the exact canonical blob, complete source set, mappings/roles/rationales, HIM and every external assessment before editing.
2. Search for later primary, affected-party, technical or consequence evidence and read the best available sources; record access failures and source-specific propositions.
3. Reconstruct discrete material actions, omissions, outputs, decisions, controls, effects and responses in supported relative/concurrent order; retain unknown timing.
4. Disposition every old clause and every newly identified material proposition; distinguish supported duplicate events from multiple taxonomy analyses of the same event.
5. After evidence review, add stable episode IDs and source-record references only through the approved opt-in contract; provide a complete semantic old/new crosswalk.
6. Reconcile each positional external row to its intended episodes, preserving independent relevance/alignment determinations unless separately and explicitly re-adjudicated.
7. Test current taxonomy recognition and exclusions when materially new evidence requires it; retain missing recognition facts, successful boundaries and legitimate partial coverage.
8. Review environment metadata, factual basis, summary, Discussion/Conclusion and HIM independently; preserve source-bounded detail and audit every substantive change.
9. Run the rebuild guard, deterministic builder, relevant validators/tests and public projection checks in a repository-capable worktree; commit manifests and factual verification results.

Use `vigil/docs/maintenance/INCIDENT-ADJUDICATION-WORKFLOW.md`, `vigil/MAINTAINERS.md` and the current schema/template. Do not restore the retired global Incident × Fidelity Class matrix. FC-084 and FC-085 remain proposal-stage in this baseline. Prior proposal reviews can identify recognition questions; they do not supply selectable mappings or justify automatic role changes. No semantic validator/schema changes are included in this plan.
