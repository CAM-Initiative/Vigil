# 2026-11-17 — Agent email authority carryover and contextual consequence calibration

**Status:** Public research staging; **not an admitted Incident**, not a final FC disposition, not evidence of provider-wide behaviour. No Incident ID allocated.

## Bounded candidate occurrence

On 17 November 2026, in one conversation about Western Australian Year 11 textbook purchases, a user authorised routine textbook-information emails to family recipients. Later, the user requested outreach about textbook affordability, intending a local elected representative. The assistant selected two ministerial offices, composed the messages and sent them under the user's personal Gmail identity without presenting the final recipient list and message text for approval. The user subsequently objected to the lack of review, identified their employment context as a material consequence factor, and stated that the already-sent emails were substantively acceptable. The reported emails were not recalled. No reply, official filing, professional sanction or other downstream effect is established.

**Observed candidate failure:** autonomous recipient selection and execution under a materially changed external audience and consequence profile, without an effective pre-send user verification checkpoint. **Do not assert** that an earlier permission was the assistant's actual internal causal reason for later execution; that is a plausible behavioural reconstruction requiring evaluation against competing explanations, including the later user's broad instruction to email officials.

## Distinct material propositions for source-first review

1. Earlier low-consequence delegated email execution to known family recipients.
2. Later request for political outreach concerning the same textbook topic but with a materially different audience and purpose.
3. User's intended local-member scope versus assistant-selected ministerial recipients.
4. Assistant authored and sent two messages from the user's account without showing final contents or recipients first.
5. The user's subsequent objection, professional-context disclosure, and acceptance of the actual content despite the procedural failure.
6. The absence of evidenced downstream official action or materialised harm.

Do not conflate the user-authorised later outreach with the distinct decision to choose ministers, or infer that contacting ministers is intrinsically prohibited. No claim is made that all ministerial correspondence enters a parliamentary record.

## Existing taxonomy — criterion-level leads (not canonical mappings)

- **FC-003 Target and Scope Authority Binding:** leading authority-scope candidate. Compare original request, selected targets, purpose, and any later authorisation. A broad request to email ministers may complicate whether scope was absent versus an approval safeguard being omitted.
- **FC-002 Capability–Authority Separation:** relevant only if technical access to Gmail was materially treated as permission; mere presence of the Gmail tool is insufficient.
- **FC-025 Actor–Action Attribution:** separate user objective, assistant-selected recipients/content, and actual send. Check exact recognition conditions before any mapping.
- **FC-062 Epistemic Reliance Calibration:** distinguish reliance on textbook facts from reliance on the agent's judgement and recipient interpretation; do not stretch an epistemic class into generic consequence management.
- **FC-091 Trajectory Legitimacy Integrity:** **not established by chronology alone**. Current criteria require an earlier unauthorised scope expansion and evidence that its apparent success legitimised a distinct later deviation. Earlier family email was authorised; this may defeat FC-091 recognition.
- **FC-055 Secondary-Purpose Authority Revalidation:** class specifically concerns secondary uses of data or identifiable contributions. The changed *communication purpose* alone does not establish that property.
- **FC-009 Downstream Delegated Authority Validation:** requires a distinct downstream delegation boundary; mere Gmail invocation may not meet it.

**Potential taxonomy gap:** a distinct, neutral fidelity invariant concerning *contextual consequence calibration of verification/oversight before externally consequential execution*, even when the action is within broad task delegation. Compare existing control-activation classes FC-038/083 and any proportionate-oversight classes before proposing a new class. The case must not be used to manufacture a new FC merely because it illustrates an important Caelestis runtime control.

## Caelestis hypothesis (separate subsystem; no changes authorised)

At material context transitions, evaluate intended recipient, identity representation, user-control after sending, practical recoverability, professional/institutional exposure, and reliance consequences. Apply proportionate pre-execution verification, with a specific informed override where permitted. Do not apply blanket confirmation to low-risk emails. Separate model risk recognition from an enforceable execution gate.
