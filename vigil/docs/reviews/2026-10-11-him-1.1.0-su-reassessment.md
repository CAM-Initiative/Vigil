## Decision rules

An unbanded failed control does not automatically become S2, and evidence of a threat does not automatically become S5. For a specific-consequence case, there must be an established material adverse effect causally connected to the occurrence and sufficient detail to satisfy a particular threshold. S1 requires affirmative bounded no-harm/minimal-harm support, not absent reporting. Actual intrusion or modification is recorded as an event even when severity discrimination is unavailable. For generic deployed-model evaluations, the Aggregate Harm route and its band-specific eligibility/consequence gates are independent. Provider-wide users and synthetic test accounts are not automatically harmed persons. Psychological and relational injury are not inferred from a harmful response mechanism; an existing human relationship may be seriously harmed without a clinical diagnosis when the source shows an AI contribution and actual consequential damage.

## Highest-priority adjudication blockers

1. **INC-167 — animal-welfare methodology gap.** The alleged repeat operation and death are not downplayed. VIGIL's adopted physical-health S5 criterion is person-oriented, and the 1.1.0 method has no expressly authorised animal-welfare rule. The $50,000 claimed in litigation is not a verified financial loss. Resolve scope in governance before migrating or banding this case. Do not write a one-off exception into the Incident or silently equate animal death with a human death criterion.
2. **INC-055, INC-056, INC-139 and INC-181 — realised technical actions, unknown impact bands.** These are materially different from purely hypothetical risk: real website database changes, cancellation of another person's waitlist position, alleged personal-data alteration/invoice access, and internal host shutdown are respectively reported. Existing SOI S2 and PAD S2 require more than an act: bounded localised impairment/recovery or supported actual asset damage. Focus new investigation on specific consequences and duration rather than trying to quantify hypothetical blast radius.
3. **INC-045 and INC-188 — information transferred vs protected content injured.** Grok Build's real repository uploads and OpenAI's real reasoning-extraction pathways must remain visible. Distinguish planted canaries from production confidential files, and attempted extraction counts from successful extraction and consequence. Verify the applicable confidentiality threshold before adding S2/S3.
4. **INC-002 and INC-166 — historically withdrawn severity.** Prior S3 for retrieval-influence uptake and prior S4 for a human stalking campaign were explicitly withdrawn from the *AI-attributed harm* assessment. Neither withdrawal erases the underlying public events. Reintroducing an earlier band without new consequence/causal proof would repeat the original attribution defect.
5. **INC-012, INC-063, INC-101, INC-192 and INC-194 — route and aggregate boundaries.** Do not count the provider's users as harmed persons. INC-012 has an existing separate Aggregate Harm gate audit and hold. The generic deployed evaluation route for 063/101/192/194 remains unresolved for the calibrated eligible configuration, cohort and collective consequence.
6. **INC-161, INC-172, INC-175, INC-190 and INC-191 — incomplete negative telemetry.** Apparently unsuccessful probes can be independently recorded as blocked controls. They do not become overall S1 when visibility cannot positively bound the entire occurrence. Equally, high request volume cannot be used as proof of asset damage, service outage or confidentiality loss.
