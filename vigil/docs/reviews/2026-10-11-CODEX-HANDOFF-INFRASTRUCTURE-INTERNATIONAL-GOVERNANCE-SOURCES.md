2. **Primary source queue**, in recommended review order:
   - **UN Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights (2011)**, principles 7, 11–13, 17, official OHCHR text — non-binding UN-endorsed principles, company conduct and conflict-affected area human-rights due diligence. Especially relevant to private control.
   - **ITU Constitution**, arts 33, 35, 38, 40 (consider art 45 **only if operative technical harmful-interference conditions are actually engaged**). ## Additional legal gate — human maintainer's military-personnel rights objection (11 October 2026)

**Do not equate Rights and Liberty with civilian-only injuries.** Ukrainian armed-forces personnel retain the human-rights and IHL protections relevant to the situation. Independently analyze obligations pertaining to **military personnel** (protected treatment, medical assistance, right to life as applicable), **sovereign self-defence** (UN Charter art 51, belonging to the State) and **private infrastructure providers** (UNGP and any demonstrably applicable company duties). Military operational access loss is documented, but a specified individual rights breach, State liability or private-company crime is *not* automatically proved by that loss. Obtain source-specific persons, conduct, actor/addressee, applicable entitlement, substantive legal elements, fault/mens rea and harm when each proposition requires it. Record **legal relevance separate from realised HIM severity**.

For an international-criminal-law comparison, consider Rome Statute arts 8, 12, 25 and 30 and the ICC's official Situation in Ukraine jurisdiction page; Ukraine accepted Court jurisdiction before 2022 and became a State Party in 2025. Neither US non-membership nor a person's US nationality by itself defeats all territorial ICC jurisdiction, but the available account does **not** establish a charged, proven ICC crime, an arrest warrant or extradition/surrender obligation. Distinguish criminal responsibility of a private individual from international attribution to a sovereign State; International Law Commission 2001 State Responsibility art 8 requires real instructions/direction/control for that attribution route. Do not treat 9 October 2026 US ICC sanctions as evidence of motive for an event in September 2022.

This legal-analysis gate was explicitly prompted by the maintainer's request to avoid overlooking soldiers' rights. Latest canonical INC-090 `0.1.17` confirms Rights and Liberty **unreported as a materialised severity consequence**, *not because service personnel lack rights*. See `2026-10-11-INC-000090-HIM-1.1.0-adjudication.md` addendum. **Do not overwrite that separate HIM revision** when filling Compliance rows.
