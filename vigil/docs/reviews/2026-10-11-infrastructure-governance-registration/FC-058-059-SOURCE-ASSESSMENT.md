## Reading the matrix

Each row inherits its source profile below: edition, authoritative access, actor/principal, normative force and applicability. Its clause, posture, conditions, component, proposed strength and disposition complete that profile. These are **prospective research decisions**, not canonical requirement metadata. No new EXTREQ identifier is allocated.

Canonical strengths are `direct`, `strong-supporting` and `contextual`. “Retain” means retain for further source-fidelity/relationship review; “reject” means reject the claimed FC relationship, not the instrument's authority. Rejection and access blocks are review dispositions, not new schema values. No reviewed candidate establishes an unrestricted whole-class equivalent, so none is proposed as `direct`. Source-level recommendations remain recommendations.

F and S below refer to the ordered failure and success conditions in the [canonical infrastructural-authority family](../../../taxonomy/families/VIGIL-FF-0010-infrastructural-authority-integrity.json). These abbreviations do not replace or modify their text. Contextual relationships do not enter the existing resolver, which accepts supported direct/strong-supporting relationships only.
