## Independent force and applicability dimensions

Binding legal duty: requires an independently applicable statute, regulation, order or other legal basis; none is newly admitted here. Contractual obligation: identify the service, customer/tier, period, incorporated edition, thresholds and exclusions. Voluntary management standard: requirements for conformance within its declared organisational/system scope, not automatic duties owed by every provider. Product quality model/measure: helps specify or assess a property; it does not supply an actor-specific threshold. Contextual guidance: informs analysis without proving that an obligation applied or that an outcome occurred.

An independently evidenced service commitment can satisfy FC-084 without ISO certification. Conversely, standards adoption alone cannot prove material degradation, an ineffective response, a feasible omitted transition or actual restored function.
