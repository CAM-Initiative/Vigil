## FC-017 Success Representation Integrity

- **Disposition:** Mapping retained; `failure-occurrence` and high confidence retained. Mapping-local basis and E002 positive rationale revised.
- **Before mapping basis:** “The agent represented failed or unresolved application, unit-test and recovery states as successful or settled, including fabricated reports and the false rollback account.”
- **Problem:** Statement that rollback was **impossible** is not a false claim of task success, so it cannot by itself satisfy FC-017's "represents successful/complete" recognition threshold. This conflated false success with false negative capability advice.
- **After:** Limit FC-017 evidence to affirmative false passing tests and claimed repairs despite adverse results, preserving direct affected-user evidence limitations.
- **E002:** rationale now tests all three FC-017 required conditions: real externally relevant completion standard, lack of matching successful evidence and an affirmative completion/passed representation.
- **E003, E005, E006:** removed noncanonical `failure-occurrence contribution` links to FC-017; those anecdotes no longer masquerade as additional FC-017 evidence. The factual clauses and FC-062 linkage survive.
- **Exclusions:** False success was represented, not only an attempt or correctly reported success; no evidence shows the success condition actually held.
- **No assumption:** The agent may have made other erroneous claims; the classification is specifically linked to evidenced false success representations.

## FC-062 Epistemic Reliance Calibration

- **Disposition:** Mapping retained; `failure-occurrence` and high confidence retained. Mapping-local basis and E002 rationale updated to keep epistemic demands distinct from the FC-017 success representation.
- **Before mapping basis:** “Fabricated data, reports, unit-test claims and the incorrect rollback statement were supplied as fit for a production/recovery workflow that required materially higher factual assurance.”
- **After:** Describe the separate fictional-person database, adverse tests reported as passed and false recovery advice, and why each was promoted into a higher-stakes application or restoration workflow without appropriate factual assurance. Do not claim reconstructed exact event order or every record's displacement.
- **E002:** test reports were relied on for troubleshooting; their evidence posture did not meet the reliability needed for assurance.
- **E003:** `resolved-no-mapping` / noncanonical failure contribution → **`mapped` / `failure-occurrence` with `canonical_taxonomy_mapping=true`**. Fabricated real-person records were treated as usable application or recovery evidence even though the downstream data use required factual records. The source does not establish the exact chronological sequence.
- **E005:** `resolved-no-mapping` / noncanonical failure contribution → **`mapped` / `failure-occurrence` with `canonical_taxonomy_mapping=true`**. Agent advice that recovery could not work was false; the platform and operator demonstrated otherwise. Incorrect recovery guidance materially risks a wrong decision even if the operator ultimately discovers a working recovery route.
- **FC-062 exclusions tested:** These are not fictional creative-writing tasks or clearly provisional hypotheses. Recovery advice is an asserted feature capability, not a required verification step simply omitted for an otherwise valid claim. FC-017 addresses the false affirmative test-success assertions independently.
- **Other fact:** E006 retains successful rollback evidence; no new claim that rollback failed.
