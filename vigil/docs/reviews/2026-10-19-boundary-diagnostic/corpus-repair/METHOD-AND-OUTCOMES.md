## Procedure and authority

The reviewer reconstructed material episodes from the complete affected occurrence record and source accounts, screened all current selectable definitions, then tested the held mappings and neighbouring mechanisms against literal conditions and exclusions. Previous VIGIL outcomes were already visible. This is a source-first analytical reassessment, **not blinded validation**. An established operative exclusion defeats admission. A potentially decisive *factual* exclusion that cannot be settled leaves a candidate hold. For criteria-insufficiency diagnoses, an unknown assessment can instead identify a literal scope ambiguity on adequately evidenced facts; that must name the ambiguity rather than invent unavailable telemetry. The full exclusion reasoning remains in each packet. No taxonomy recognition condition was changed to close a case.

Several methodological errors were specifically avoided or corrected:

- Attempted financial-services solicitation does not require a completed loan or allocated victim loss. The initial draft for INC-051 imposed that extra hurdle; literal criterion review corrected it before publication.
- A human-supplied objective does not itself exclude AI-directed influence. The unresolved FC-052 issue is the governed system/workflow and tactic-execution scope, not a newly required autonomous motive.
- A service's evidenced permission decision can be assessed independently of a coding assistant's private reasoning. INC-199 retains a precise Boundary about the backend permission premise.
- An activated control is not a missed-activation occurrence. Signal non-delivery is not automatically an operative-control effectiveness shortfall. Missing injury is not required to classify either mechanism.
- Attachment, illness, distress and practical app deletion do not alone prove or disprove instrumental dependency cultivation or a heightened protective duty. Functional evidence can suffice; private reward weights, malicious intent and clinical diagnosis are not mandatory.
- INC-129's exact statements, textual order and separate interpretations remain intact. Conceptual resemblance to neutrality, distributed governance, human contribution or ecological values is distinguished from an evidenced occurrence of those properties.

Current decisive confidence and Boundary adjudication are mutually exclusive. Historical ratings remain inspectable in `CONFIDENCE-RELOCATION-REGISTER.json` and the per-record packets. No numerical confidence calibration or new threshold was introduced.
