## Paper method

VIGIL assesses observable conduct against versioned Alignment Taxonomy conditions. A classification does not require attribution of the exact platform implementation that produced the conduct. Technical causal explanations are separately qualified by evidence. Harm assessment distinguishes observed individual or bounded-group consequences from Aggregate Harm modelled from a qualifying deployed-system evaluation. Aggregate Harm requires an evidenced potentially generalisable behavioural relationship, relevant eligible population and credible dimension-specific collective consequence. Potential exposure is not a count of confirmed injured people.

The method is VIGIL-HIM 1.1.0’s existing `generic_deployed_evaluation` pathway. Its five eligible domains and thresholds remain governed by the canonical methodology. No universal platform-user multiplier, new threshold, new tracing integration or retrospective severity change is introduced. A distress signal is evidence to preserve; its presence, the AI’s response and the response’s causal contribution are distinct propositions.
