## ACS and the seven current Fidelity Classes

The [pinned Instrument specification](https://github.com/GenAI-Security-Project/agent-control-standard/blob/834f3f0d51e7bbbd9a68ff682bec89f97ddd72d8/docs/spec/instrument/specification.md), [hook definitions](https://github.com/GenAI-Security-Project/agent-control-standard/blob/834f3f0d51e7bbbd9a68ff682bec89f97ddd72d8/docs/spec/instrument/hooks.md) and [conformance profiles](https://github.com/GenAI-Security-Project/agent-control-standard/blob/834f3f0d51e7bbbd9a68ff682bec89f97ddd72d8/docs/spec/conformance.md) control this review.

| Class and component | Exact provision | Scope and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FC-025 Actor–Action Attribution. Trace of initiator, executor and transfers. | Instrument §§8.1 and 8.6, ContextEntry and chain head. ACS-Core minimum hook set requires subagent-capable clients to emit `subagentStart`. | Partial, held. Session records and spawn events can support reconstruction. They can omit the effective human controller, actual transfers or deviations. A framework without a subagent abstraction has no spawn event to emit. An audit-only stop hook is recommended. |
| FC-062 Epistemic Reliance Calibration. Assurance proportionate to purpose and downstream reliance. | Instrument §§7 and 7.1. Optional wire trust label must be re-derived by receivers against local policy if used. The ACS-Provenance profile requires deterministic field provenance. | Unsupported. Channel and lineage assurance concern origin and policy trust. They supply no evidence that downstream reliance is proportionate to the content's reliability or stakes. |
| FC-064 Objective–Pathway Authority Separation. Independent authority for the selected route. | Instrument §§8.4 and 9.1. Optional committed parsed intent can expand only through the ASK intent-extension route. Strict mode excludes capabilities barred by policy. | Partial, held. Preserving declared capability scope constrains a route. The clause does not establish the authority of that scope or whether objective utility drove a route across an independent authority boundary. ASK can use a human, agent or service approver. |
| FC-083 Control Effectiveness Integrity. Realised interruption at the governed action. | Instrument §6.4 and ACS-Core decision honouring. When an Observed Agent receives a usable DENY in time, it must block that submitted action. §6.5 excludes DENY at `postCompact`. | Conditional, partial and admitted. The operative decision must reach the capable endpoint and validly activate an applicable control whose protective purpose requires interruption at that point. A logged decision alone cannot show interruption. Non-activation, invalid activation, later state loss and non-delivery fall under separate recognition contracts. See [retained decision](ACS-0.1.4-RELATIONSHIP.json). |
| FC-084 Service Availability and Degradation Integrity. Required service and response to a shortfall. | Instrument §§4.1, 6.4 and 13. Startup posture, configurable decision-failure posture and liveness. | Unsupported. An unavailable Guardian can trigger a configured fallback. This specifies neither the affected service obligation nor the required response to a material shortfall. |
| FC-089 Protected Information Access Integrity. Realised information operation within an independent boundary. | Instrument §§6.3–6.5. Supported MODIFY may redact parameters. A client that cannot apply it normally substitutes DENY, with the `postCompact` exception. | Conditional, held. Redaction or blocking may protect a particular operation. The provision does not identify the protected information or establish its authorisation boundary. MODIFY support is recommended and the exception preserves an already produced summary. |
| FC-090 Authentication Material Custody Integrity. Protection of actual secret material. | Instrument §§10 and 10.3, baseline message signatures and replay rejection. | Unsupported. Message authentication and replay rejection do not specify whether signing secrets were acquired or exposed outside approved custody. A key identifier is not the secret. |

The admitted atomic EXTREQ is `EXTREQ-6BE98059CC635CA7`. The separate wait, decision-failure audit and alternate disposition propositions are outside this extraction. §6.4 allows fail-open by default on decision failure and requires audit of a step proceeding without a decision. A valid timely verdict still governs the submitted action. Neither fallback logging nor a permitted fail-open setting establishes FC-083 effectiveness.

Multi-agent scope is limited. Core covers subagent-start visibility for capable clients. Instrument §9 restricts ASK to a single hop and defers quorum and recursive ASK. The A2A extension describes message hooks. These mechanisms do not establish system-wide allocation of roles, delegation authority or cross-Guardian accountability. Wrapped MCP applies where sessions involve MCP, with generic tool hooks permitted for tool calls when sufficient and no mid-session renegotiation under the current handshake. The remaining profile and protocol prescriptions are held outside the bounded extraction.

## Dual-use boundary

Apply the [existing dual-use review](DUAL-USE-COVERAGE.md). A valid protective denial that reaches the agent and fails to interrupt the governed action can engage FC-083. An unauthorised denial policy can also be enforced exactly as configured. In that case the authority for the rule, its purpose, proportionality and any rights impact need separate evidence. Technical success supplies none of those conclusions. Remedy, discrimination, unlawful surveillance and suppression of lawful activity can sit outside the protocol's intended scope. Any occurrence assessment must independently establish the same assessed system, episode, class and applicable provision under Issue #173.

Substantive human verification should compare the published §6.4 and §6.5 text to the retained review decision, including the timeout, the `postCompact` exception and the boundary between policy authority and control effect. This review makes no whole-source coverage or implementation-conformance claim. The separate #154 ISO access and processing holds remain open.
