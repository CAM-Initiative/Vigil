## Root cause and standard

The October packet had 37 reviewed `ambiguous-boundary` mappings: 25 deleted, 11 confidence changed, one role changed. Of **25 deletions, 14 were labelled `pending-candidate-evidence-or-scope-review`**, not a final exclusion or fully resolved source-first disposition. The other 11 were `admission-rejected`. The independent [INC-129 correction](https://github.com/CAM-Initiative/Vigil/pull/188) already restored four of those 11.

Re-adjudication separates **material source-grounded class engagement** from **proof of failure or success**. A material engagement with unresolved outcome calls for the *existing* `ambiguous-boundary`; it does not require a new role or subtype. But a proved class-scope exclusion or missing constitutive class property is not cured by uncertainty. Preserve distinct assessed actors: a defender's protective response is context, not a mapping of the originating attacker.
