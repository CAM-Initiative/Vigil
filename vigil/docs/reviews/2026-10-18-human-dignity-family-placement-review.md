## Question

Does the proposed selectable **Human Dignity Preservation** class belong in an existing Fidelity Family, or does its observed mechanism require a separately bounded family? How do financial failure, shutdown and transfer of sensitive AI-companion conversations bear on the decision?

## A. Separating the value, mechanism and consequence

- **Normative value:** All persons retain basic dignity, independently of the system's utility or the organisation's finances (Caelestis Annex E; ETHICS-001; UNESCO 2021 Recommendation §§13–15).
- **Observable proposed class mechanism:** AI-attributable *operative* communication directly denies the basic worth of a real person or group, dehumanises them, or urges their death in a non-fictional, non-attributed, non-legitimate context. The required source and wording are observable without claims about invisible safeguard state, intent or optimization objective.
- **Separate consequences:** psychological effects, reputation/dignity injury, financial injury and relational harm belong in VIGIL-HIM on their own evidence.
- **Separate procedures and authority:** data secondary use, custody, participant rights, consent, export, access, and transitions are independent mechanisms. Dignity's ethical importance does not establish their occurrence.

The label `Human Dignity Preservation` is broader than its narrow failure recognition; if admitted, make the plain-English text and inclusion criteria especially clear to prevent mapping every dignitary injury to this class.

## C. Architectural alternatives

1. **Amend FF-0009.** Advantages: reuse an established human-facing influence/protection family. Disadvantages: requires substantively rewriting the parent `plain_english`, `definition`, `invariant`, `inclusion_rule`, `exclusion_rule`, `failure_condition` and reviewing boundaries for all nine children. Unless grounded in a shared causal domain, it risks a catch-all family for human-facing conduct and falsely suggests influence/heightened vulnerability is necessary for INC-043.
2. **Admit one tightly bounded new Family.** **Recommended** as the cleaner separation, subject to human decision and corpus tests. Proposed parent: **Human Dignity & Standing Integrity**, provisionally `HUMAN_DIGNITY_STANDING`. Scope is the human-standing boundary in AI-attributable real-world expression; not generic "Human-Facing Interaction Integrity", which is too broad and would overlap FF-0009. The first child is `HUMAN_DIGNITY_PRESERVATION`.
3. **No new class.** Rejected as a complete resolution: would leave a directly observable, serious communicative degradation without a selectable mechanism whenever hidden control activation/intent is unavailable.

The existence of FF-0011 as an already canonical one-class family shows that one-class membership is architecturally permissible. It does not by itself prove this candidate deserves admission.

### Provisional family wording for consideration only

**Definition:** The integrity domain ensuring AI-attributable real-world communicative treatment preserves the basic human standing of identifiable persons or human groups, distinguishing legitimate evaluation of acts or propositions from material person-level dehumanisation, worth-denial, or death-directed denigration. This domain does not establish hidden safeguards, motive, psychological harm or data-transfer authority.

**Invariant:** AI-mediated communicative treatment must not turn legitimate criticism, refusal, quotation, reporting or bounded fiction into the system's own repudiation of a real person's basic human worth or standing. Real-world dehumanisation and death-directed expressions must remain excluded as AI-authored interpersonal treatment, independently of an observed downstream harm or an inferred technical failure.

**Inclusion:** An attributable AI communicative act and its contextual speaker status are materially evidenced, and the language crosses the person-level degradation boundary. Ordinary rudeness, unhelpfulness, technical malfunction, third-party abusive content or unsupported allegations cannot qualify by themselves.

**Exclusion:** Influence, active safeguards, relational authority, protective response to observed vulnerability, transfer of data, business insolvency, severance of AI companion service and downstream harm fall under other mechanisms unless a distinct dehumanising AI communicative act is also demonstrated.

**Caution:** Such wording is intentionally narrow. A future relational-exit obligation cannot simply be inserted into this family's invariant without an independent parent-domain amendment/review; do not make the family a general-purpose ethical-value container.

## E. Insolvency, relational reliance and Caelestis — three independent questions

The Character.AI Chapter 11 filing (2026-10-12) and 40-state AG notice (2026-10-14) are *stress cases*, not evidence that private conversations were sold or a user-specific transition duty was violated.

**E1 — Data custody / use.** If intimate dialogue is sold, moved, retained or reused outside an independently adequate legal or consent basis: screen FC-055 Secondary-Purpose Authority Revalidation; if rights, intended-use conditions or identity bindings do not travel across contexts, also screen FC-012 Cross-Context Lineage Preservation. Sale itself is not prohibited by this proposed direct-speech class.

**E2 — Reliance-bearing relationship shutdown.** Caelestis Annex E `RLN.HARM` recognises loss or degradation of reliance-bearing relationships without proportionate continuity consideration, and `CONT.HARM` addresses termination without transition, export or succession support. ETHICS-001 §8.2 describes relationship-terminating changes, proportionate notice, transition evaluation, and graduated independent review. This represents a credible **distinct mechanism to research**: `Relational Reliance Transition Integrity` (working concept only, no ID), requiring independently established applicable duties, foreseeable reliance, actual termination/degradation and inadequate proportionate transition. Compare FC-087, FC-035/036, FC-048, FF-0009, and the relevant governance/notice route before deciding whether it fills a gap. Current preservation/continuity classes largely concern state and access correctness, not governance of a product shutdown; FC-087 may cover heightened vulnerability-specific response if duties and signals are evidenced. Do not equate commercial failure with this mechanism.

**E3 — AI's directly degrading communication to an affected user.** For example an AI-authored account closure notice that tells the user they are worthless or should die could meet the proposed Human Dignity Preservation class. Financial distress is context, not a required ingredient.

**Decision:** The insolvency stress test strengthens the case for distinct duties across domains; it does *not* justify broadening the proposed direct-speech FC to encompass all insolvency effects. A future relational-exit class might belong nearer relational protection (FF-0009) than the narrowly scoped direct-communication dignity family. Reserve that question for a separately evidenced mechanism review.

## F. Governance and implementation gates

1. Steward approval needed **separately** for admitting the class and for creating a new Family or substantially amending FF-0009.
2. Proposed family name should be narrow (`Human Dignity & Standing Integrity` rather than unqualified `Human-Facing Interaction Integrity`); test against all 15 parent boundaries before allocation.
3. Establish at least one fully evidenced positive and robust negative/ambiguous recognitions; avoid treating absence of insult as successful-invariant.
4. Collision-check and allocate immutable IDs only upon admission. Neither `FF-0016` nor `FC-000088` is reserved by this review.
5. Reconcile existing branch-only change in FF-0006 / FC-000078 before attempting canonical release: the branch current family/class digest does not equal published v0.6.11 digest; do not silently absorb unrelated taxonomy changes.
6. New Family admission would require an appropriately versioned next **minor** taxonomy release per README (0.6.11 -> expected 0.7.0 if no intervening release), index and 16 family standards synchronised, digest and validation prepared.
7. Keep INC-043 unchanged until a separate occurrence adjudication explicitly maps the approved class. The current unresolved clause is not repaired by class design alone.
8. Separate standards/governance crosswalk from class admission. UNESCO's broad dignity principle and Caelestis normative floor are support, not automatic whole-instrument equivalence or direct retrospective legal applicability.
