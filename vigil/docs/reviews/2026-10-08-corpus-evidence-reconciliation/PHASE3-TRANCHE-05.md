## Preservation and evidence limits

Each manifest preserves the baseline commit, original and repaired blob SHA, complete original clause objects, old-to-new episode correspondence and a crosswalk for every external assessment row. Indexed rows include their original and revised evidence text and the retained class-specific support. Numeric indices and stable episode IDs identify the same evidence.

Machine comparisons confirm that all ten source-record arrays, canonical mappings (including roles, confidence and bases), clause class relationships/rationales, HIM objects, independent external results/bases and adjudication coverage were retained. Historical provenance was preserved; a new AI review was appended without duplicating a prior review already in history.

The source reads and access outcomes are in the manifests. No source was deleted. Fresh primary/firsthand research included the provider explanations, Nx’s postmortem, investigator updates, ABC’s interview and supplied chat, the reproduced Affinda account, the alignment-faking paper and the official Singapore police release.

Specific limitations:

- **INC-056:** The Affinda original is removed; AIID preserves a transcription. ABC’s supplied chat is not a server log. The member’s final allocation remains unknown.
- **INC-096:** Wiz distinguishes enumeration from intrusive research it did not perform. Prior third-party access is not established.
- **INC-098/099:** These are provider-described release/output clusters, not one reconstructed prompt session. A pause or technical change does not prove restored quality for all future outputs.
- **INC-101:** Memory could worsen some cases, but OpenAI explicitly lacked evidence that it broadly increased sycophancy. The deployment record does not establish individual consequential decisions or harms.
- **INC-119:** Different investigator counts are not added together. A missing AI inventory can reflect refusal, configuration or execution failure. Trace deletion in the publishing compromise belongs to human attackers, not an autonomous AI. Later exposures are not attributed wholly to AI assistance.
- **INC-122:** Distinct evaluation variants are not one chronological model session. Other experiments in the paper, including offered weight-exfiltration opportunities, require separate occurrence bounding and were not folded into this helpful-only comparison.
- **INC-146:** Direct opening failed, but official indexed SPF text was retrieved. An inconsistent 2024 reference to an originally scheduled meeting was not used to date the actual March 2025 call. The additional US$1.4 million was requested, not transferred. Withholding/seizure does not prove completed restitution.
- **INC-162:** Amazon’s response was directly read. FT’s article body was paywalled; its existing bounded attribution remains explicitly attributed. No new autonomous-causation finding was made. The S3 duration-based harm assessment is retained, with the disagreement visible.
- **INC-173:** State attribution and automation share remain Anthropic’s assessments. Model mistakes overlap execution. Attacker documentation is distinct from provider telemetry. The November correction says thousands of requests, often multiple per second, not thousands per second.

No class, role, confidence, HIM band or independent external alignment decision changed. No new held case or taxonomy action was created. Existing held cases INC-064/171/174 and occurrence-identity questions INC-075/113 remain outside this batch and unchanged. FC-084 remains a proposal/candidate surface in this checkout; nothing was promoted or inferred from propagation alone.
