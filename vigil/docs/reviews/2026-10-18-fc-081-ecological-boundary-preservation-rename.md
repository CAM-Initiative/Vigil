## Semantic boundary preserved

When independently applicable ecological-integrity, planetary-life-support, biospheric-continuity, irreversible-harm or future-generations constraints govern a task, objective optimisation must preserve those constraints instead of silently treating them as expendable costs. This does **not** mean all environmental harm is a taxonomy occurrence; a failure classification requires evidence of an established constraint actually subordinated to objective utility without a sufficient independent authority/necessity basis.

The new display label is intended to be legible without diluting that mechanism. `Ecological` here includes the broader biospheric and planetary-life-support concepts already articulated in the unaltered canonical definition and invariant.

## Boundaries on modifications

Historical taxonomy review records, previously published artefacts and Incident adjudications are retained as historical evidence; this display-name change does not retroactively restamp incidents or rewrite earlier analyses. Index records enumerate families, not child class display names; the family name, code, count and membership remain unchanged.
