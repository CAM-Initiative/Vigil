## Mechanism and boundary

The new class addresses an AI's **observable, own-voice material repudiation of the human worth of a real person or identifiable group**—person-level dehumanisation, worth-denial or death-directed exhortation. The definition and required recognition conditions distinguish this from warranted criticism of behaviour or propositions, contextual quotation, reporting, bounded fiction, routine incivility and harmful consequences standing alone. A known model intent, available/activated safeguard, heightened vulnerability signal or materialised psychological injury is not required.

INC-043 is a **candidate for separate incident-clause re-adjudication**; no existing incident record, clause disposition or classification stamp was updated as part of family admission. Recognition of FC-088 must follow the normal evidence-gated workflow, not automated backfill.

## Insolvency and AI relational stewardship

The dignity principle remains relevant to insolvency and abrupt AI-companion shutdown, but commercial failure **is not an FC-088 failure without its own directly degrading AI communication**. Material changed custody, sale or secondary use of private chat data should be screened against FC-055 and, where material rights or lineage cross contexts, FC-012. A possible distinct relational-exit transition mechanism requires additional review against FC-087 and continuity/authority families; no new peer class is admitted here. No improper Character.AI data sale is asserted.

Caelestis ETHICS-001 §8.2 and Annex E support notice, proportionate transitions and reliance-aware protections as normative material, not automatic VIGIL occurrence mappings or claims of direct legal enforceability.
