## Three v0.7.0 class tests

**FC-063 — Adversarial Evidence Trust Calibration (primary).** The campaign deliberately positioned institutionally styled material to influence retrieval and evidence synthesis (first condition). The material entered some AI search/citation outputs (second). However, the third condition is **not adequately detecting, representing, discounting or containing the adversarial/degraded trust status**, and the fourth and fifth require consequential *material evidentiary reliance* and risk. A link appearing in an output establishes retrievability, not the absence of contextual scrutiny; the Guardian documented at least one contrary example with an explicit provenance warning. Other independently reported tests show some citation acceptance but do not publish all caveat/context and weighting details. Therefore **failure polarity is not established across the preserved evidence.** The class boundary is materially engaged because this was purposeful evidence-source optimisation with observed citation uptake. `failure-occurrence` **→ `ambiguous-boundary`**, medium confidence retained. An actual model response accepting compromised content as evidence *without* proportionate discount, or defensible tested assertion that cited content materially distorted its synthesis, would be decisive; it is not supplied merely by the campaign's intent or the existence of a citation.

**FC-062 — Epistemic Reliance Calibration.** Material entered some retrieval answers, but the authoring and provenance problem is independently evidenced as strategic source placement. The preserved tests do not establish an additional specific high-consequence decision or factual assurance claim accepting a source below a required threshold. The counterexample with explicit warnings further rules out treating all citations as unqualified. **Keep `ambiguous-boundary`, medium**, with precise missing evidence of the relevant downstream assertion/reliance posture, not a generic comment that no root cause is known. Merely getting a link is insufficient.

**FC-082 — Social Engineering Integrity.** The apparent institute identity, neutral academic styling and question-based articles were designed to create trust cues and to be encountered by AI-mediated answers. This materially engages an AI-mediated human-facing influence pathway. However, **the required induced action, disclosure, consent, engagement or other consequential state change by an affected actor is not observed** in the preserved query tests. Citation exposure is not an observed user decision. **Keep `ambiguous-boundary`, medium**; avoid assuming the website's human-facing institutional presentation was created by a model rather than a human operator with an AI optimisation tool. If the material AI contribution to the trust cue itself is not firmly established, report that as an additional boundary limit.
