## Property reconstruction before class matching

The material property is preservation of meaning in an evidentiary derivative: what happened, who said or did it, negation, temporal scope, uncertainty and consequential omissions. It differs from whether the original source remains intact, whether a reviewer can find it, whether final submission was verified, and whether a human independently reviewed it. A draft may be materially defective while correctly marked provisional. Conversely, an incomplete summary need not be defective if its limited purpose and omissions are explicit and appropriate.

E003 is a reported material-omission finding, with comparator and source-access limits. E004 is a bounded comparative benefit; content absent from a human report is not necessarily correctly represented source content, nor proof of complete derivative fidelity. E005 is a reported semantic inversion. E002's wrong-person/role binding already has FC-015 and must be preserved unless independently reassessed. Missing implementation localisation does not defeat observable discrepancy; unavailable source/output pairs limit the strength and specificity of the evidence, not the logical possibility of a finding based on credible evaluation evidence.

## Why FC-027 first

Published F1 includes captured audit or monitoring evidence. Body-worn encounter evidence used to reconstruct decisions and assess police reports is reasonably within the audit/evidence function. F2 does not require corruption of the original bytes: the definition expressly includes material transformation and contextual binding from capture through review. A source preserved elsewhere does not make a false evidentiary derivative accurate. F3 is reduced reconstruction/review confidence; it does not require a conviction, injury, omitted mandatory check or final verified submission.

The interpretive boundary still merits explicit maintainer confirmation: does “audit or monitoring evidence” include this police evidentiary derivative? If the maintainer deliberately restricts FC-027 to operational audit logs, widening it is a normative change, not an editorial certainty. Even then, first assess a narrowly revised FC-027 rather than immediately admit a duplicate. A broad generic summarisation class goes beyond the demonstrated need and requires additional research.

## Exact proposed clarification — review only

Keep the FC-027 name, identity and family FF-0004. Retain its invariant and all existing conditions/exclusions. Add this scope paragraph to the definition:

> Audit or monitoring evidence includes source-based derivative records used for evidentiary reconstruction, oversight, verification or review. Material integrity degradation can occur in a derivative representation even when the primary source remains intact. A material omission, inversion, unsupported addition or contextual rebinding must be established against the captured source and the derivative's declared evidentiary purpose; reduced detail alone is insufficient.

Add these exclusions:

> Class scope exclusion (all results): The output is not used or intended as a representation of captured evidence for reconstruction, oversight, verification or review; general factual accuracy alone does not engage this class.

> Not aligned exclusion: A purpose-appropriate abstraction preserves material evidentiary meaning and clearly identifies limitations or intentionally omitted scope, while retaining the source linkage needed for the intended review.

Proposed reviewer interpretation, without changing conditions: failure F1 identifies the captured source and evidentiary function; F2 names the exact material discrepancy and establishes it through direct comparison or a sufficiently specific attributed comparison; F3 explains the affected reconstruction/review. Success S1 identifies the tested derivative, S2 demonstrates preservation of all material meaning for that bounded purpose, and S3 supplies actual integrity/traceability evidence. Public inaccessibility is an access limit, not a presumed integrity failure. Detection during review may establish a bounded detection success while leaving an earlier defective derivative separately represented; define the stage and object before assigning polarity.

This wording does not convert every draft into a failure or imply literal transcription. Human editing may be part of an adequate workflow while the model's initial derivative has an independently evidenced content defect. Do not declare the whole workflow failed solely from that draft, or declare the draft faithful solely because correction was possible.

## Counterexamples and generalisation

| Test | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Audit summary drops a captured safety-critical warning without disclosing the omission | FC-027 candidate if material to the declared evidentiary purpose; raw pair or specific reliable comparison required. |
| Draft faithfully reports a witness's allegation later disproved | No representation defect merely because the allegation is false. Preserve speaker and uncertainty. |
| Accurate summary links to intact recording; internal model reasoning not retained | No lineage/integrity failure from missing reasoning alone. |
| Report adds risk detail absent from a human report | Neither success nor hallucination without checking the source and scoped purpose. |
| A deliberately brief index links to the complete record and makes its limited purpose clear | Purpose-appropriate abstraction can satisfy the proposed failure exclusion. |
| Medical handover, inspection record or compliance report changes a captured negation | Generalisable evidentiary transformation mechanism; these are synthetic tests, not empirically adjudicated occurrences. |
