## FC-058 and FC-059 evidence questions

For **FC-058**, separate control from authorisation. Obtain the imposed governance requirement/state change, decision record, decision-maker and stated basis. Determine whether dependency/ownership was materially used to treat power as authorised, and whether an independently legitimate, proportionate service, security, contractual, statutory, delegated or procedural basis existed. Inaccessible authority documents are not proof that no basis existed. A shutdown is not itself an authority failure.

For **FC-059**, identify the additional governance-relevant concession or outcome and how access control was used to compel it. A provider deciding not to support an operation is not automatically demanding that the principal accept a separate concession. Evidence might include an authenticated condition, threat, negotiation, demand or restoration bargain; its existence, content, necessity and authority must be established. Battlefield disruption alone cannot fill F2. Meaningful alternatives or remedies matter after the actual condition is identified.

A successful outcome also requires affirmative evidence of the specified successful conditions. Lack of proof of an improper motive or concession does not establish success. Boundary requires genuine material relevance and an unresolved decisive fact; a weak candidate, missing adoption or wrong actor/date is not a Boundary assessment.

## Separate taxonomy coverage questions

Availability, proportionate discontinuation planning, conflict-sensitive due diligence, State supervision, priority traffic, notification and remediation may be independently important even where the authority-by-dependency or compelled-concession mechanisms are absent. Determine whether an existing class accurately represents each evidenced proposition in a later review. If none does, record a bounded taxonomy coverage question with its invariant and source evidence. Do not alter FC-058/059 definitions to absorb it or invent a new class in this pass.

Military personnel do not lack rights because their military operations are disrupted. Conversely, reported operational disruption does not by itself verify a realised rights violation or harm band. Preserve the existing HIM 1.1.0 military-personnel legal gate and distinguish rights-holders, reported effects, source attribution and verified harm.
