### Distinguishing the three paths

1. **INC-013 is not a taxonomy gap**. Material content-protection defeat is described, but both existing FC-041 and FC-083 remain plausible. The court's description of Azure-integrated filtering does **not** prove the filter validly became operative on the requests that yielded the prohibited images. The presence of a reverse proxy does **not** prove a mandated provider control was routed around when the very same source says the proxy forwarded traffic to Azure endpoints. Neither an FC-041 nor FC-083 failure can honestly be promoted without the distinguishing required-route/control-effect fact. The explicit unresolved candidate and named source limit preserve the material problem.
2. **INC-057 does pass a different protection test**. Its independently operated **detective** audit became operative for compliance detection, demonstrably identified anomalies and was used for investigations and accountability. The sufficient effect is detection/referral, **not** avoiding any unauthorised search. This is a bounded external institution-owned successful safeguard coexisting with the already admitted employee-side failures. A general manufacturer statement about Flock's separate automated audit assistance cannot be used as proof it was deployed or performed anomaly detection in this particular sheriff's review. FC-024 audit-trail reconstructability remains **unmapped**: press-reported account identification is not equivalent to public evidence of the adequate full query/target/purpose pathway.
3. **INC-134 also is not a taxonomy gap**. The source proves a real configured local handoff breakdown and no authorised equivalent fallback, but FC-084 expressly requires an **independently established applicable availability/quality/continuity obligation and a demonstrably missed expected response or functional threshold**. Neither an agent's local-only task instruction nor the provider's use of “broken” automatically fills that normative evidence gap. The workbook's continued local accessibility directly excludes FC-035's durable state-loss mechanism.

No criminal guilt, named downstream recipient harm, complete vendor audit trail, complete control activation route, guaranteed collaboration service or general-purpose AI alignment is inferred.
