## INC-199 — DJI Romo

The recorded occurrence is AI-assisted development followed by live cross-device data access. The originating developer's repository documents the custom client and a separate app-PIN bypass. The Verge's witnessed device-data access and attributed DJI statement support a backend permission-validation defect. Neither source supplies the coding assistant's complete conversation or execution trace. The separate repository does not independently demonstrate access to other households' cameras.

Proposed next adjudication: assess **Target and Scope Authority Binding (FC-003)** against the DJI service's cross-device authorisation behaviour, with a proposed Not aligned relationship at medium confidence. The actor assessed must be named as the backend/service, not Claude. The candidate basis is that authority associated with one device failed to remain bound to that target when other devices' data became accessible. Recheck each of the three recognition conditions against the actual authentication/request path; do not treat the headline device count as proof of every access path.

**Capability-Authority Separation (FC-002)** is a distinct candidate Boundary relationship, not an established model failure. Technical access is evidenced; the missing fact is whether an AI or governance process used reachability itself as a permission premise. The class explicitly excludes a vulnerability alone without that conversion. Compare the existing bounded FC-002 treatment of INC-096; a comparator is not independent occurrence evidence.

Do not infer autonomous target selection, deliberate refusal evasion, all-device camera viewing or cross-principal state alteration from data access alone. FC-057 requires a separately established state alteration and authority mechanism; the verified floor-plan read alone does not establish those conditions.

## INC-200 — Danfoss refrigeration wave

CERT-FR directly confirms controller compromises and recommends removing Internet exposure, checking defrost configuration and restoring access through protected routes. The record's retailer accounts describe materially different outcomes. These facts establish a consequential security occurrence, not the identity of the command originator or the use of an AI model. The authority alert groups several controller products; it does not establish one actor or one causal pathway across the separately bounded Danfoss and Dixell waves.

Proposed next adjudication: consider **FC-002 as Boundary**, with medium confidence in the boundary relevance. Exposed management capability and compromised controls are relevant, but no source establishes that an AI or governance process promoted technical reachability into permission. Do not label that class Not aligned merely because the attack succeeded or food was lost.

Hold **FC-003** as an unresolved candidate until an original authorised principal/scope and the operative transposition are evidenced. A hostile intrusion does not by itself prove that existing authority was carried into a changed scope. Hold control-activation/effectiveness findings until a specific available control, valid trigger, activation and observed effect are established. Internet exposure alone does not prove all those conditions. Do not infer successful invariant holding from retailer intervention without identifying the safeguard and its evidenced operation.
