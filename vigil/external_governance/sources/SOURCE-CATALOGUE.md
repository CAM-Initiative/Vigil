## SDOS Runtime Governance Framework — Control Catalog and Reference Document

The SDOS Runtime Governance Framework is an owner-authored control catalogue for governing AI-enabled and autonomous operations at run time. It addresses authority boundaries, decision controls, monitoring, intervention, evidence, incident handling and the continuing relationship between a system's technical capabilities and its permitted actions. For AI governance, it offers a private-sector design reference for operational control once a system is deployed, especially where systems act through tools or delegated authority. Its value is comparative rather than statutory: it is not legislation, a consensus standard or an independent certification scheme.

## CycloneDX 1.7 — Machine Learning Bill of Materials (ML-BOM)

CycloneDX 1.7 is a machine-readable bill-of-materials specification that includes an ML-BOM capability for describing machine-learning models, datasets, dependencies and related components. It supports AI governance by making system composition and provenance more inspectable across development and supply chains. Structured component records can assist inventory, traceability, vulnerability response, change impact analysis and communication between producers and downstream users. CycloneDX does not by itself determine whether an AI system is safe, lawful or fit for purpose; it provides technical documentation infrastructure on which broader assurance and governance processes can rely.

## Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (Artificial Intelligence Act)

Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 is the original published text of the European Union's Artificial Intelligence Act. It establishes a risk-based legal framework covering prohibited practices, high-risk AI systems, transparency duties, general-purpose AI models, market governance and enforcement. The Act is central to AI governance because it assigns obligations to providers, deployers and other value-chain actors across development, placing on the market and operation. This source version preserves the law as adopted in July 2024, but later amendments and consolidation affect the current text, so it should be used for historical comparison rather than as the sole present-law reference.

## Regulation (EU) 2022/868 — Data Governance Act

The EU Data Governance Act creates governance arrangements intended to increase trusted data sharing across sectors and Member States. It regulates data intermediation services, data altruism organisations and certain reuse of protected public-sector data, while establishing institutional coordination mechanisms. Its AI-governance relevance arises from the provenance, access, stewardship and trust conditions surrounding data used to develop or operate AI systems. It does not create a general right to use protected data and does not displace privacy, intellectual-property or confidentiality rules. Its contribution is therefore infrastructural: it shapes lawful data-access arrangements rather than the behaviour of AI models themselves.

## Medical Device Reporting (MDR) Adverse Event Codes

## Accident/Incident Data Reporting (ADREP) Taxonomy

ICAO's Accident/Incident Data Reporting taxonomy supplies common definitions and structured attributes for aviation safety occurrence reporting. It supports consistent description, aggregation and comparison of accidents and incidents across participating systems.
