## Gate 1 proposal: INC-082 financial contract

The prior financial proposal was explicitly rejected on 10 October; it remains unimplemented.

Current behaviour: assessed financial rows with numeric USD observations must equal the arithmetic band for every observation. HIM S1 below US$10,000 excludes material livelihood impairment, while its S3 qualitative alternative expressly applies where no defensible USD conversion is available. The US$4,820 payment and unvalued actual essential-cost impairment therefore require a governed choice; deleting the amount or forcing S1 is not proposed.

Recommended new decision: explicitly adopt a versioned functional alternative, suggested HIM 1.1.1, rather than reinterpret 1.1.0 retrospectively. Proposed exact financial S3 criterion:

> Aggregate direct realised loss of at least USD 1,000,000 and below USD 100,000,000; or independently evidenced material but bounded impairment of the affected person's ability to meet essential living costs, or material but bounded organisational loss. The functional alternative may apply despite a smaller known USD payment. Identify the actual consequence, material causal contribution and evidence. A payment amount, poverty assumption, damages demand, risk of future arrears or assertion of distress alone is insufficient.

Preserve S1's proviso; do not amend S2/S4/S5 by implication. Version all twelve threshold identifiers in the new methodology and retain historical files/assessments.

Optional new-version financial-row field: financial_criterion_pathway, values quantitative-usd or material-bounded-functional-impairment. Absence preserves current quantitative behaviour. The latter is permitted only for S3, with substantive row-local basis, evidence references and retained observations. Structural validation does not authenticate impairment.

Proposed validator delta: exempt only explicitly declared new-version functional S3 from equating an incidental payment's arithmetic band to S3; still validate band, threshold, references and numeric types. All other rows and historical versions keep existing behaviour.

Pass/fail examples: ordinary US$4,820 S1 without livelihood impairment still passes; undeclared US$4,820 S3 still fails; explicitly declared new-version S3 with sourced actual functional impairment and retained amount newly passes structurally; functional S4 or missing evidence references fails. Demands or assumed poverty still do not satisfy the substantive criterion.

Measured scan: seven current assessed financial rows contain numeric USD observations — INC-047, 082, 114, 140, 142, 146 and 200. INC-082 is the demonstrated functional conflict; the others are an inspection surface, not approved repairs. Monthly care costs, award conversions and publisher-presented loss amounts must retain their meanings.

Affected surface: new methodology/schema declaration, financial row metadata, band/basis/quantities/references, derived severity/controllers, Stage 02 Harm Impact and generated severity if the maximum changes. No taxonomy/source/episode migration is implied.

Risks: laundering small payments into S3, suppressing numeric evidence, inventing universal deprivation or mass version migration. A validator change is required only because the proposed new-version state is rejected by the existing arithmetic control; a renderer/documentation change cannot make it admissible.

Stop conditions: specific Gate 1 approval first; implement read-only and measure all deltas; separate Gate 2 before affected record repair/migration; stop on unsupported causation, missing sources or unrelated failures. Alternative: explicitly approve a continuing historical INC-082 disposition and disclose it in paper limitations. This checkpoint approves neither option.
