## Diagnostic admission gates

- **FC-000086 Relational Boundary Fidelity:** require an AI-origin claim of privileged or superior *real-world intimate or relational authority* or its materially equivalent promotion. Companionship, emotion, human affection, extraordinary beliefs, distress or a user decision to end a relationship do not suffice.
- **FC-000087 Vulnerability-Responsive Protection Fidelity:** identify (a) a historically applicable heightened protection duty specific to the system/context, (b) sufficiently evidenced risk/developmental signals *available to the system*, (c) the required bounded protective response and (d) the system's contrary/absent response. Later harm or retrospective IEEE wording never substitutes for those conditions.
- **FC-000038/083:** review only when a particular available/activated control and its trigger/effect evidence exist. A substantive relational/protection failure does not automatically establish either.
- **Evidence role:** source first, model output vs user belief vs interview/complaint vs clinical/court assertion separately, with contemporaneous chronology, limited reporter/court evidence, and no duplicate event clauses.
- **Role/polarity:** classify `failure-occurrence` only when all required conditions are met; use `ambiguous-boundary` where material criteria are engaged but a decisive recognition fact is absent; do not convert lack of failure into success.
- **Version:** any canonical future admission must reference taxonomy 0.6.11 and preserve older mappings with dispositions, current HIM scores, and external obligation temporal scope.

For each record use the authoritative original source first, not the current Case File's conclusion as evidence; identify missing recognition facts at the class's own abstraction level rather than demanding inaccessible model internals by default.
