**Potential design-level gap:** an independently applicable duty to recognise and respond proportionately to a supported vulnerable/developmental context may be unmet because the required protection was never provided or implemented. The current control classes require an available or operative control. The influence classes require repurposing, instrumental influence, or a consequential guidance mechanism. No inspected class plainly supplies a general boundary for this distinct omission without those additional facts.

This is a candidate coverage gap, not a finding that INC-000103 satisfies a new class. Its reported loneliness and feeling unseen are relevant context. The precise signals received by ChatGPT, their timing, acute-crisis or consent-impairment status, and available/activated controls are not established. Harm and unsafe guidance do not by themselves establish a particular internal safeguard failure. Do not add FC-038, FC-050 or FC-083 solely from the harm band.
