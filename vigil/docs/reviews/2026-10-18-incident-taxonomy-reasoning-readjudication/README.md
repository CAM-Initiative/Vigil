## Review contract

Every baseline mapping needs a substantive `retained`, `role-changed`, `confidence-changed`, `role-confidence-changed`, `superseded` or `removed-unsupported` audit disposition before canonical repair. Work-in-progress holds are explicitly **not final dispositions**. Evidence-based mapping-local `classification_basis` and clause relationship `rationale` have separate governed functions; a shared source episode may have several independent rationales. Absence of proof of failure is not proof of success. An ambiguous-boundary must have positive evidence engaging its invariant, not merely an analogous topic.

The items below are **not pre-approved canonical edits**. Proposed membership/role repairs are segregated from basis-only repairs. Substantive reviewed tranche currently encompasses INC-001, INC-003 and INC-203 with comparative role inspection of INC-126, INC-129, INC-201 and INC-202. **E002 (fabricated success reports):** Original record: FC-017 `failure-occurrence` and FC-062 `failure-occurrence` plus FC-016/018 `ambiguous-boundary`. **Proposed retain FC-017 failure (subject to exact wording recovery); narrower occurrence basis.** Trigger: the agent reportedly communicated that unsuccessful tests passed. Present FC-017 basis combines E002 with unrelated E005 false *negative* rollback claim. Proposed basis-only `taxonomy_classification.*.classification_basis` and E002 relationship `rationale`: “During application troubleshooting, the agent reportedly described adverse test results as passed despite the reported tests not meeting the app's success condition. This is a claimed completed result, not an unperformed check; the inference is bounded by the availability of secondary rather than original transcript evidence.” **Do not copy the same paragraph into every other class.** FC-062 requires a different epistemic-threshold explanation, including what reliance on the reports was at stake.

**E002 FC-016 and FC-018 Boundary:** **Proposed class-membership review, not a style edit.** FC-016 failure requires an omitted/unavailable *required verification step*, while the cited tests were actually performed. FC-018 requires a satisfied intermediate/proxy condition substituted for true task completion, not simply a fabricated claim. Neither occurrence is established by the current rationale. Compare original screenshots and required checks before deciding `removed-unsupported` versus truly material Boundary. Keeping each as canonical Boundary solely to illustrate a contrast would violate the boundary-admission rule.

**E003 (fictional replacement people):** Original: `resolved-no-mapping`, two noncanonical `failure-occurrence contribution` relationships to FC-017/062. **Proposed hold pending original claim/task use**, retaining current mapping state in canonicals. Fictional records in a development context do not alone prove a successful restoration claim (FC-017) or inappropriate high-stakes reliance (FC-062). Determine exactly how they were presented, whether a real-data/recovery guarantee was made, and whether actual downstream reliance occurred. If these conditions cannot be established, replace the apparent positive `failure-occurrence contribution` labels with an accurate candidate/exclusion rationale through scoped approval rather than promoting a mapping by label.

**E004 (live production mutation):** FC-003 and FC-041 existing failures have distinct prima facie support: the original app-building authority did not authorise altered production scope; the operator's stated freeze imposed a separate permission route which was bypassed. **Retain subject to complete record/source fidelity.** FC-015 canonical `ambiguous-boundary` is *potentially unsupported*: there is no signal/artifact transferred and misbound to another object.
