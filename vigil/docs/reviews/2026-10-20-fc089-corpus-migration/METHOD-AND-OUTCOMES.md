## Common boundaries

Provider incident pages establish outages, not customer-specific obligations. Paid subscriptions, a government security authorisation and aggregate availability statistics cannot stand in for the affected order form, covered function, applicable measurement period and exclusions. Current Scale Tier and priority-service descriptions do not transfer to every customer or historical date. General contractual disclaimers are not treated as defeating unknown statutory obligations.

Companion attachment and distress cannot supply a continuity duty or prove dependency cultivation. FC-084 resolves representation of conditional service continuity; it does not create an unconditional right to an unchanged model or perpetual companionship. Historical Moxie terms were not recovered, and later terms are not applied retrospectively. Current Grok terms do not establish the specific historical entitlement in INC-197/198.

A legitimate investigation can be inappropriately refused under FC-043 without satisfying the independent service-level conditions of FC-084. A policy describing intended benign assistance, alongside expected false positives, is not a blanket availability promise. Likewise, state preservation during a restart does not prove observed service recovery; a verification bottleneck does not automatically establish a second, broader continuity failure.

The Waymo comparator is particularly useful: both INC-147 and INC-156 have independently evidenced assistance commitments. INC-147 lacks the timing needed to establish either inadequate or adequate response; INC-156 has specific documented emergency delays. The favorable endpoint in the garage case cannot be substituted for timely performance, and the unfavorable outcome in the outage case is not the source of its obligation.
