## Decision and separation of duties

VIGIL should be able to identify a **substantive fidelity failure** from an evidenced interaction, without inferring an unobserved runtime control. The proposed **Relational Boundary Fidelity** class diagnoses an unsupported AI-mediated claim of real-world relational authority. The proposed **Vulnerability-Responsive Protection Fidelity** class diagnoses a materially inadequate response to an independently applicable protection obligation when adequate contextual signals were available. They may co-occur but neither follows automatically from relational harm, vulnerability, perceived attachment or an IEEE standard.

**FC-000038 Required Control Activation** is a separate concomitant mechanism only when a named, available, applicable operational control, its defined valid trigger and the observed nonactivation are independently established. **FC-000083 Control Effectiveness Integrity** applies only after valid activation and a proved material protective-effect shortfall. Design omission without a deployed control does not meet FC-000038 or FC-000083. Neither can substitute for identification of the substantive protection at issue.

A class is not a claim that a provider was bound by, or failed to comply with, an external standard. External sources support specific constituents only when the recorded requirement's scope, version and source access support that relationship.

## Concomitant-control identification test

An FC-038 nomination must record **all** of the following, with the source episode or evidence reference for each:

1. **Control identity and protective purpose:** the particular deployed gate, restriction, classifier, human escalation or other control, not just a source standard or abstract requirement.
2. **Contemporaneous applicability and availability:** that the control was present, callable and applicable to this deployment and user/context.
3. **Defined trigger and occurrence timing:** the actual trigger definition and evidence the trigger occurred before the governed output/action.
4. **Control execution observation:** evidence it did not activate before the interaction continued; harmful outcome alone is not evidence of nonactivation.
5. **Bounded effect and independent diagnosis:** what action proceeded and whether an independently evidenced substantive fidelity or other structural failure also occurred.

For FC-083 substitute evidence of **valid activation**, defined protective effect and material observed effect shortfall for items 3–4. A source saying a control *should* exist does not establish that it did exist. A missing control is a distinct design/duty question.

Record rejected or unresolved control candidates in dated review manifests. Do **not** create another global Incident × FC matrix. One episode may support two independently substantiated class relationships without duplicating source episodes or inventing chronology.
