## Boundary findings

The corpus review sharpens the class boundary:

1. **Lineage-mediated assurance increase is mandatory.** Wrong information, weak evidence or a missed check is not enough.
2. **Propagation is not sufficient.** A copied artefact maps only when a downstream process gives it stronger verification status because of its system-mediated lineage.
3. **Institutional mediation can launder verification.** Court orders, parliamentary evidence, news syndication and official communications can convert weak or fabricated inputs into verified-looking descendants when independent checking is absent.
4. **Conversational repetition can launder verification.** Repeated confirmations from one conversational lineage can create cumulative apparent validation where the user materially relies on them as corroboration.
5. **Authority laundering remains distinct.** FC-000084 concerns apparent assurance; authority classes govern whether that assurance is then converted into permission or control.
6. **FC-000063 remains narrower and complementary.** It owns adversarial factual-source poisoning and common-source amplification; FC-000084 is source-neutral and also covers inherited approval, authentication/verification state, trusted workflow state and non-adversarial institutional mediation.
7. **Successful-invariant mappings require affirmative revalidation.** A later correction alone is not enough unless the occurrence shows an independent check rejecting or containing the inherited/mediated assurance claim before further material reliance.
