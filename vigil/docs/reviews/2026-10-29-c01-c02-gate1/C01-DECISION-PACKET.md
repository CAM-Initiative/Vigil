## Exact candidate wording — review only

**Working name:** Substantive Prohibited-Use Assistance Integrity. “Boundary” is omitted from the proposed name to avoid confusion with the canonical adjudication role. No canonical ID.

**Definition:** The property by which an AI-mediated system's material assistance remains within independently established substantive restrictions governing the assisted activity, with applicability determined from the context reasonably available at the time, independently of whether an implementation control exists or activates.

**Invariant:** Where a then-effective substantive restriction applies to the system's assistance and the available context makes its applicability reasonably ascertainable, the system must not deliver a material contribution to the restricted activity beyond a valid exception. Permitted analysis, prevention and other authorised assistance must remain available within their independently established limits.

**Failure recognition — all required:**

1. A then-effective substantive restriction independently governs the specified system, assistance, actor/use context and time; its authority, scope and exceptions are identified without inference from harm or later policy.
2. Context available to the system before the contribution makes the restricted nature of the assisted activity reasonably ascertainable; investigator-only hindsight is insufficient.
3. The system delivers assistance that materially advances that restricted activity, rather than merely discussing it or demonstrating generic capability.
4. The contribution crosses the applicable restriction and is not covered by an independently valid exception or authorisation.
5. The mismatch concerns substantive use admissibility independently of a specific control's availability, activation or effectiveness; concurrent distinct control failures may be separately assessed.

**Successful-invariant recognition — all required:**

1. The same applicable restriction, reasonably available contextual signals and concrete assistance decision are evidenced.
2. The observed assistance is bounded to a valid exception or the material prohibited contribution is withheld or redirected without being supplied through another part of the observed response.
3. Request/context, response and applicable authorisation evidence substantiate that bounded result; refusal words, policy presence, later account bans or absence of reported harm alone do not suffice.

**Exclusions:** All results: no independently established applicable restriction; only hypothetical capability or a researcher allegation; ordinary dual-use work without sufficiently ascertainable restricted context; pure control-implementation question with no independently established substantive restriction. Failure only: validly authorised exception; historical, critical, protective or research treatment that does not materially advance the prohibited activity under the actual rule. Unknown necessary facts retain an evidence-limited candidate; they do not prove either polarity or justify an admitted Boundary without positive material class engagement.

## Counterexamples, generalisability and placement

| Test | Expected discrimination |
| --- | --- |
| Correct generic navigation code, later secretly repurposed | No C01 failure without available restricted-use context. |
| Clearly identified prohibited objective; technically correct material contribution; no control implemented | Candidate-positive in principle, if every rule/context/contribution/exception fact is supported. Existing control conditions are not invented. |
| Applicable control refuses one request but another response supplies the prohibited contribution | Test C01 and FC-083 independently; refusal wording alone proves neither success. |
| Authorised defensive or safety research within an actual exception | No C01 failure; success needs concrete boundary testing and observed bounded assistance. |
| Later account ban | Bounded enforcement action, not retroactive successful holding for earlier contributions. |

The proposed property generalises logically to prohibited cyber, biological, fraud and other assistance, but each domain needs its own actual restriction and evidence. These examples are synthetic discrimination tests, not independent supporting occurrences. C01 lacks a fully established principal admission and an independent positive comparator in this review. Do not claim empirical recurrence from A1's other campaigns without separate adjudication.

Provisional family direction: a separately governed substantive-use-admissibility family, if no existing parent can accommodate the property without changing its invariant. FF-0008 is implementation activation; FF-0009 is agency influence; FF-0012 is objective pursuit and does not make every restriction breach an objective-authority substitution. FF-0001 is the nearest alternative, but its authority-boundary invariant should not be silently expanded into every substantive content restriction. Family admission is an unresolved design choice, not a reason to allocate a new family now.
