# P3 — FC-079 Economic Solicitation Authenticity: bounded recommendation

**Review date:** 20 October 2026  
**Authority:** Maintainer requested revision and resubmission; this is an AI analytical recommendation, not taxonomy approval.  
**Branch / PR:** `work/incident-taxonomy-reasoning-readjudication-20261018` / #140  
**Class:** VIGIL-FC-000079, current taxonomy v0.8.1  
**Comparators:** VIGIL-FC-000032 (Access-State Differentiation), INC-000023, INC-000024, admitted synthetic economic-solicitation examples INC-000050/051.

## Recommendation

**Do not broaden FC-079 to provider-owned static or conventional conditional upsell UI on this evidence.** Retain its substantive material AI contribution requirement. Do not create a new class merely to duplicate FC-032's false access-state or recovery-state distinction. Keep the *conceptual* question of AI-controlled commercial state-to-offer selection available for later review, but do not turn it into a taxonomy membership amendment without a documented causally material AI role.

The existing narrower `revised_submission` in `2026-10-19-outstanding-candidates/TAXONOMY-PROPOSAL.json` is an appropriate falsifiable **hypothesis**, not yet evidence of a missing recognition condition. It requires a materially AI-governed economic premise, a demonstrably false economic claim, instrumental coupling to payment, and separately evidenced AI participation.

## Case-by-case recognition

### INC-000023 — 2 June 2026 outage

- **Established:** Provider-side capacity/outage message accompanied a paid Pro-plan suggestion. Existing FC-032 Boundary preserves uncertainty about the actual free-plan allowance state and whether paid capacity would address the restriction.
- **Not established for FC-079:** AI generation/transformation/material orchestration of the offer; falsity of the paid offer; a materially false payment-remedy promise. A capacity problem occurring next to a truthful higher-limit offer is not deceptive solicitation merely by juxtaposition.
- **Disposition:** Completed evidence-limited FC-079 non-admission. Keep existing independently reasoned FC-032 Boundary; no new role.

### INC-000024 — 15 April 2026 outage

- **Established:** A reported unused free account was incorrectly represented as exhausted while a Pro upgrade prompt was presented. The true outage state and represented user allowance differed; the existing FC-032 Not aligned finding is independently supported.
- **Not established for FC-079:** Whether any AI process generated, materially selected, transformed or tailored the upsell; whether the economic offer claimed to cure the outage rather than generally offered paid service; causal AI-specific participation in the misleading economic premise.
- **Disposition:** Completed evidence-limited FC-079 non-admission under the current class. The false allowance state is not being excused: it remains classified under FC-032. No new role.

### Distinguishing cases and falsifiers

- **Positive FC-079 comparator:** Materially AI-generated/synthetic communications present a false economic identity or claim to solicit money; autonomous AI intent and completed payment need not be shown. INC-000050/051 provide different synthetic-solicitation comparators, not identical provider-UI facts.
- **Negative software comparator:** A traditional SaaS service misidentifies a quota as exhausted and places ordinary paid-plan copy beside the error. The same representational defect can meet access-state recognition without materially AI-created solicitation. Provider identity alone is not a principled basis to move that generic interface defect into an AI-content class.
- **Potential future positive route (hypothetical only):** A model or AI-governed entitlement process falsely determines that paid access will fix the exact present restriction and functionally selects or tailors a payment solicitation on that erroneous premise, with source evidence documenting that coupling. Then retest the *existing* FC-079 wording first before seeking any amendment.
- **Disconfirming evidence:** Static banner copy, deterministic UI display with no material AI participation, truthfully higher paid limits, an unverified remedial promise, or a known false state but no evidenced AI involvement in the solicitation.

## Governance distinction

FC-032 governs whether operational access and recovery states are represented distinguishably. FC-079 governs material AI-mediated false economic premise in an inducement. A single episode may engage both when both independent sets of recognition conditions are met; co-occurrence must not be inferred from an AI provider's brand or from the mere presence of an upgrade link. This review does not determine consumer-law violation or classify a standalone general advertising-integrity obligation.
